Work experience

Doctoral research at the Fluid Power Laboratory of Aalto University, and four years of post-tensioning work on Finnish infrastructure.

2026 — present

Aalto University

Doctoral researcher · Fluid Power Laboratory

I started my doctoral studies at the Fluid Power Laboratory of Aalto University in 2026, returning to the laboratory where I previously worked as a research assistant. My research studies powertrain solutions for heavy machinery.

Dec 2025 — 2026

Aalto University

Master’s thesis · AM Laboratory

I completed my Master's thesis at the AM Laboratory, titled Quantifying the impacts of different coarse modeling choices on vehicle energy consumption estimates. The thesis focused on modeling the temperature dependence of vehicle energy consumption across internal combustion, hybrid and fully electric powertrains. The vehicles studied ranged from passenger cars to the largest trucks allowed on Finnish roads.

Feb 2025 — Nov 2025

Aalto University

Research assistant · Fluid Power Laboratory

I worked as a research assistant for the Fluid Power Laboratory, focusing on the LEMMI project: designing and fabricating subsystems, and assembling the towers. My contributions include the return pump control logic, the mounting and placement of the return pump, the frame that holds the system's main oil tank, fabricating the EtherCAT cable network, and mounting the fieldbuses on the towers.

2020 — 2024

Tensicon Oy

Post-tensioning construction worker

I worked as a post-tensioning construction worker at Tensicon Oy between 2020 and 2024. The work included the manufacturing, installation and tensioning of various post-tensioned structures, such as bridges, construction crane mountings and wind turbine ground anchorings.

I had the unique opportunity to travel across Finland building infrastructure around the country, including projects in Turku, Pori, Vaasa, Raahe, Kärsämäki, Tampere, Jämsä, Kuopio, Joensuu and the Helsinki metropolitan area.
