01

FluPo robot

FluPo is a gadget inspired by a vision of a mascot for the hydraulics laboratory of Aalto. FluPo embodies the essence of a benign seal that barely manages to make its way around. The idea is a work in progress, and will hopefully gain attention and support among the Mechatronics research group at Aalto.

FluPo runs an ESP32, two N20 DC motors, an L9110S dual channel H-bridge motor driver module and a 1S LiPo. The robot is controlled remotely over Wi-Fi using ROS 2 and an Xbox controller.

  • ESP32
  • ROS 2
  • Wi-Fi teleop
  • L9110S H-bridge
02

RC drifting gyro

An Arduino based RC drifting gyroscope that produces realistic countersteer. The system uses an MPU6050 IMU to read the yaw rate of the RC car, and uses this to command the steering servo to countersteer, replicating the behaviour of a real car. The project turned out very well, competing with commercial products in terms of performance. The countersteer logic is based on PD control of yaw rate.

  • Arduino
  • MPU6050
  • PD control
  • Servo steering
